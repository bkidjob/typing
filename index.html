Touch Typing Built for Developers,
Powered by Offline AI

The privacy-first Chrome Extension that analyzes your physical typing patterns and integrates local language models (Ollama, LM Studio) to adapt to your skills in real-time.

Add to Chrome — It's Free & Offline-First
A Seamless, Personalized Journey
Step 1

See Your Performance in Real-Time

Every keystroke counts. Our integrated physical keyboard heatmap visualizes your typing speeds and error patterns directly as you type.

  • Dynamic Overlays: Color-coded keys highlight your active strengths and trouble zones.
  • Ergonomic Guidance: Receive real-time prompts detailing next keys and exact finger positions.
  • Immediate Response: Track live stats like CPM, Accuracy, Errors, and Elapsed time on the fly.
Step 2

Acquire Deep Contextual AI Insights

Don't just memorize the keys—understand the source code you are practicing. Our Context Insights panel streams explanations directly inside the editor.

  • Local Execution: Queries are sent entirely to your machine via local engines like Ollama.
  • Tailored Perspectives: Configure the LLM to explain concepts as a Python Expert, CS Tutor, or more.
  • Zero Latency: Immediate, line-by-line updates as you finish active typing segments.
Step 3

Import or Generate Tailor-Made Lessons

Breathe new life into your practice routine. Load custom content manually or let our performance algorithm generate a custom lesson targeted at your weak spots.

  • Flexible Inputs: Drag-and-drop code/prose files, or paste raw text instantly.
  • Smart Generation: Auto-synthesize unique practice blocks utilizing your top trouble keys.
  • Difficulty Scaling: Calibrate word-length goals to adjust training durations dynamically.
Step 4

Analyze Your Improvement Over Time

Consistency is key to permanent muscle memory. The interactive dashboard translates your historical sessions into actionable, visual feedback.

  • Calendar Heatmaps: Track practice streaks and frequency using our GitHub-style grid.
  • Multi-Metric Charting: Cross-reference WPM, sustained CPM, and accuracy over successive days.
  • Automated Coach: Local AI metrics pinpoint your exact trend lines and offer encouraging tips.
Step 5

Tailor Ergonomics and Interface Styling

Every hand shape, keyboard layout, and visual aesthetic preference is accounted for. Calibrate the trainer to blend seamlessly into your daily environment.

  • Hand Size Calibration: Warns when complex key combos cause high-reach physical strain.
  • Alternative Layouts: Full support for standard QWERTY, Dvorak, Colemak, and hardware form factors.
  • Typing Behaviors: Fine-tune behaviors like Stop Cursor on Error, Word Wrap, and Whitespace styling.
Step 6

Lock In Your Habits Securely

Enforce physical health boundaries and control where your AI data travels. LLM connection setups and voluntary locking are fully configurable.

  • Private local URL: Configure connection URLs, credentials, and select local models easily.
  • Custom Mappings: Create custom JSON mappings to substitute complex symbols with simple letters.
  • Prevent Over-strain: Set a voluntary lockout (e.g., 5 to 60 min) to ensure deep rests between training.
